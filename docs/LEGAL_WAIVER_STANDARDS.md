# Legal Waiver & Parental Consent Standards

## 1. Statutory & Compliance Context
Event volunteerism involves potential physical labor, interactions with minors, food preparation, and public gatherings. To minimize liability for non-profit organizations, PTAs, and athletic leagues, GatherRaise implements strict digital waiver workflows.

---

## 2. Standard Waiver Templates

### A. Minor Safety & Parental Consent Agreement
* **Target Group**: Any participant under the age of 18.
* **Requirements**:
  - Declaration of Parent/Legal Guardian name and contact details.
  - Emergency contact phone numbers (primary & secondary).
  - Explicit authorization for emergency medical treatment.
  - Consent for youth to participate in supervised volunteer activities.

### B. General Volunteer Liability & Assumption of Risk
* **Target Group**: All volunteers in physical setup, teardown, staging, or athletic marshaling.
* **Requirements**:
  - Acknowledgment of voluntary participation and inherent risks.
  - Waiver of claims against the organization, school district, facility, and event organizers.
  - Confirmation of physical fitness for the chosen role.

### C. Food Safety & Hospitality Acknowledgement
* **Target Group**: Volunteers in concession stands, bake sales, food trucks, and catering.
* **Requirements**:
  - Adherence to local health department food handling guidelines.
  - Verification of no contagious foodborne illnesses within 48h.
  - Proper hygiene (handwashing, hair coverings, glove usage).

### D. Photo & Media Release
* **Target Group**: General volunteers, attendees, and performers.
* **Requirements**:
  - Permission to use event photographs and videos for non-profit promotional and social media materials.
  - Opt-out provision for parents requesting minor image redaction.

## 3. Digital Signature Integrity & Proof
Every signed waiver captures:
1. Vector stroke data / drawn signature or typed legal declaration.
2. Signer legal name and declared relationship to participant.
3. Signer email and phone number.
4. Cryptographic ISO 8601 UTC timestamp.
5. Signer IP address and browser user-agent hash.
