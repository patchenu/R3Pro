# IRS 501(c)(3) Charitable Contribution & Substantiation Standards

## 1. Statutory Background
Under Section 170(f)(8) of the Internal Revenue Code, donors must obtain a **written acknowledgement** from the recipient charitable organization for any single contribution of $250 or more before claiming a charitable tax deduction.

---

## 2. Mandatory Receipt Components
A valid 501(c)(3) acknowledgement letter generated by GatherRaise must contain:
1. **Organization Information**:
   - Legal Entity Name (e.g. *Lincoln High School Parent Teacher Association Inc.*).
   - Employer Identification Number (EIN / Tax ID).
   - Official Mailing Address & Contact Email.
2. **Donor Information**:
   - Legal Name of Donor / Corporation.
   - Date the payment was received.
3. **Financial Breakdown**:
   - Gross contribution amount received.
   - Processing fees paid (if donor elected to cover payment fees, that amount is also tax-deductible).
4. **Quid Pro Quo Disclosures (Goods & Services)**:
   - If the donor received goods or services in exchange for their contribution (such as a Gala dinner, golf round, or sponsor banner perks), the receipt MUST state the estimated **Fair Market Value (FMV)** of those goods and calculate the **Net Tax-Deductible Amount**:
     $$\text{Tax Deductible Amount} = \text{Gross Payment} - \text{Fair Market Value of Goods Received}$$
5. **Mandatory IRS Affirmation Statement**:
   - *"No goods or services were provided in exchange for this contribution other than those specified above."*
6. **Authorized Signature**:
   - Organization Treasurer or Executive Director name and signature line.
